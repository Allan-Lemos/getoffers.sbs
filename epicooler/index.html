EpiCooler uses advanced heat exchange technology to deliver fast, efficient cooling. Warm air is drawn in, passed over cooling coils that absorb the heat, and then blown back into the room as refreshingly cool air. Condensation evaporates internally, meaning no messy drain hose or water tanks.

The unit features PTC ceramic heating elements, which warm up quickly and evenly. This technology automatically regulates its temperature for safer, more efficient heating by preventing overheating while providing steady warmth.
